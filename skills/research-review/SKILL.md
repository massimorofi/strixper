---
name: research-review
description: Research a topic, compare sources, and produce a concise cited summary.
---

For research requests:

1. Identify the specific questions that need answering.
2. Gather information from multiple independent sources and record their URLs.
3. Prefer primary sources for factual claims; check publication dates when relevant.
4. Separate verified facts from inference and note conflicting evidence.
5. Summarize the findings with source links. Never invent citations or claim a
   source was read if it was not fetched.
